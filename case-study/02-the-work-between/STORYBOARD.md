# STORYBOARD - The Work Between

This video follows one ordinary repeated task. ActivityLogger helps the person see it, but the person chooses what to change.

## Story choice

- Result: practical test
- Human action: the person tests one local template after checking a possible repeated pattern
- Hook: the same useful update is built again and again
- Culmination: a before and after replay shows one possible simplification without claiming saved time
- Ending: useful change can begin by seeing the work between the work

## Video direction

- Look: light, editorial, and rhythm-led. Warm paper `#F6F3ED`, graphite `#1B1D22`, accessible muted text `#5E636B`, deep amber `#8A5A00`, and cyan `#24CFC3`. Deep amber reaches useful graphic contrast on the paper field. Cyan is decorative only.
- Type: a neutral sans for display and body with a compact mono face for action labels. Do not use Sora, Manrope, the Ciklum grid, or a Ciklum wordmark.
- Layout: a fixed work surface owns most frames. Large action words and one reconstructed UI panel take turns as the main subject.
- Signature motion: the repeated sequence `write / copy / switch / paste / send` returns at fixed anchors. A deep amber bracket appears only after the person chooses a result and marks the repeated middle they may test.
- Camera: locked throughout. Energy comes from timing, replacement, masking, and clean cuts, not zooms or flying panels.
- Rhythm: repeated task, repeated again, clear product role, safe review, possible pattern, human choice, test replay, calm close.
- Reveal rule: no scene starts full. Each action or status appears only when its spoken cue arrives.
- Stillness: Frames 4 and 8 hold. No moving trace, camera travel, floating cards, random particles, bounce, flash, or endless motion.
- Safety: 5 percent safe margin, bottom 17 percent clear for captions, AA contrast, no color-only state, and `Illustrative data` on every reconstructed screen.
